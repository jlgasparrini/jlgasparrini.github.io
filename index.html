One checkout for a group of marketplaces

GOAT Group, 2020 to 2025

GOAT Group grew by merging companies, and each one came with its own checkout. We replaced them with a single master checkout that every platform redirects to. My part was making carts work across platforms: the validations and rules each brand needed, so nothing a business team relied on got lost in the merge.

Payments were only partly integrated when I started on them. Finishing those integrations was the work I enjoyed most there.

Making search fast again

GOAT Group

Search was slow at times, and no single cause explained it. It was a mix of N+1 queries, Elasticsearch indexes drifting out of sync with the database, and very little caching. We fixed the query patterns, made index syncing reliable and added caching where reads dominated.
